      Model radar

      Pick up to four models to compare intelligence, speed, cost efficiency, how far your budget goes, and context size. Each axis is scored 0–100 against the best model in the list. The workload comes from the calculator above.

      Up to 4 models at once.

      Scoring: Intelligence and Speed are divided by the top value. Cost efficiency and Budget reach use a log scale, so 100 is the cheapest model and 0 the most expensive. Budget reach = budget ÷ API cost per request with your calculator workload. A model without a verified API price scores 0 on Budget reach and shows n/a.
